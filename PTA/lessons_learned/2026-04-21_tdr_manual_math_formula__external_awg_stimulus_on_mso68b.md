## Summary
Manual TDR impedance analysis using Ch1 voltage and two calibration points (V_baseline, V_matched) to compute ρ and Z via Math1 advanced formula. Works with external AWG70002B stimulus where built-in TDR measurement shows 'Invalid input'. Calibration is instrument+splitter specific and must be re-measured for each setup.

## Gotchas / Problems Encountered
1. Built-in TDR (MEASUrement:MEAS1:TYPe TDR) shows 'Invalid input' (returns 9.91E+37) when stimulus is an external AWG — it requires the scope's own fast edge to self-calibrate V_inc. 2. MEASUrement:MEAS1:TDR:* sub-commands (AUTOSet, STEPdetect, EQUAlize, SIGnaltype) time out on both MSO68B and DPO714AX — do not use as queries. 3. Calibration values (V_baseline, V_matched) are completely instrument+splitter specific. DPO714AX: -2.79mV/-304.7mV. MSO68B+AWG70002B: -4.6mV/-126mV. Never share between setups. 4. MATH:MATH1:DEFine? times out on DPO714AX under some firmware versions. 5. linearized impedance formula 50*(1+rho) is wrong — use exact 50*(1+rho)/(1-rho).

## Solutions
1. Use manual Math formula Z=Z0*(V-V_b)/(2*V_m-V_b-V) as primary measurement path. Built-in TDR measurement still enabled for reference. 2. Avoid querying TDR sub-commands; use *OPC? to recover after timeout. 3. Calibrate by connecting all-50Ω cables, reading V_baseline (pre-step flat) and V_matched (post-step flat) from the Ch1 waveform. 4. Use AUTOSet EXECute (not TDR-specific autoset) — works reliably on all families. 5. CH1:BANdwidth 1E9 + FILTer:OPTIMIZation FLAT confirmed working on MSO68B for simulating 4/5-series BW limits.

## Measurement Tips
V_inc = V_matched - V_baseline. On DPO714AX with 18 GHz splitter and fast edge: V_inc ≈ 302 mV. On MSO68B with AWG70002B: V_inc ≈ 121 mV. The splitter introduces 6dB round-trip loss; V_inc already accounts for this when calibrated correctly. VP=0.70 for PTFE coax. Use FLAT bandwidth filter shape for impedance accuracy; STEP shape for better rise-time fidelity on connectors.
